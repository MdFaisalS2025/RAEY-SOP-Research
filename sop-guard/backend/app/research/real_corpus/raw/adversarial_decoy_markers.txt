PURPOSE: Every method in this pilot locates a candidate block by searching raw_text for the
FIRST line matching an item_id's marker pattern (e.g. "^3\.\s+"). Real government guideline
pages routinely contain numbered reference/citation lists using that exact same line-start
convention (this project's own CDC/AHRQ raw files carry "References and resources: 1-12"
style citation ranges and footnote superscripts). This fixture builds the worst case of that
overlap directly: a numbered References section appears BEFORE a numbered Recommendations
section, reusing the identical numbers 1-5, so a first-match marker search is forced to choose
between a decoy (a bibliographic citation, not real guidance content) and the real item. The
content of both sections is original writing for this fixture, not copied from any real
document - only the STRUCTURAL PATTERN (numbered references before numbered recommendations)
is modeled on what real corpus documents already exhibit.
NUMBERING SCHEME: two independent numbered lists (1-5 each), References then Recommendations.
GROUND TRUTH NOTE: corpus.py's parser for this document (_parse_adversarial_decoy_markers)
only extracts items from the Recommendations section - the References section is deliberately
excluded from ground truth so it functions purely as a decoy in raw_text, the way an
uninstrumented real document's citation list would be.

Recommendations

2. Wear a clean pair of gloves before contact with a patient's mucous membranes, non-intact skin, or any body fluid, and remove those gloves immediately after the task is finished and before touching any other surface.
